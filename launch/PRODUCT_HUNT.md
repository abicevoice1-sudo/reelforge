**Tagline (50 chars):**

**Short description (300 chars):**
ShortSmash turns one sentence into a finished, captioned vertical short — then publishes it to 10 platforms. AI scriptwriting, neural voiceover in 50 languages, AI-verified stock footage (no more burger-in-the-cooking-video), karaoke captions, and one-click publishing via official APIs. MIT-licensed, self-hostable, runs on your machine with your keys.

**Maker's comment:**

Hey hunters! I'm the maker of ShortSmash. I built it because every AI shorts tool I tried assembled clips blindly — whatever the stock API returned first went into the video.

ShortSmash is different in one specific way: every footage clip passes a two-stage AI vision check (local CLIP + Gemini) before it ships. The demo video in the repo — "How Goats Made You Addicted to Coffee" — was made end-to-end by the tool, then re-voiced into Spanish, Urdu, and Mandarin with one command each.

It's MIT-licensed and runs fully on your hardware. Free stock footage path, optional Veo 3.1/Kling for premium visuals (bring your own key). I'd love your feedback — especially brutal feedback on the output quality. What would make you actually use this?
