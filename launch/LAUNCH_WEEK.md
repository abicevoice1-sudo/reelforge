# Noisy Launch Playbook — ReelForge

Goal: maximum real-human noise in launch week. No fake engagement, no
brigading — HN and Reddit both detect and bury it. Noise comes from a
great story told in many rooms, with the maker present.

## The story (use everywhere)

**The enemy:** the "stock-footage look." Every AI video tool ships burgers
in biryani videos because nobody checks the footage.
**The hero:** the pipeline with eyes — a local CLIP model that rejects its
own bad clips.
**The proof:** the demo video in the README was made end-to-end by the tool
itself. Lead every post with the burger anecdote.

## Day-by-day calendar

- [ ] Don't argue teardowns — ask follow-ups, note fixes publicly

## Noise multipliers

1. **The comparison post is the link magnet.** "I tested 20 open-source
   text-to-video tools" gets bookmarked and shared by people who'd never
   star a repo. It also ranks on Google.
2. **Demo > screenshots.** The biryani video plays inline on X, Reddit,
   HN (link). Silent autoplay wins — ours has burned-in captions.
3. **Reply speed is the algorithm.** First-hour replies on HN/Reddit decide
   ranking more than the post itself.
4. **One fix shipped live mid-week** beats ten promises. Pick the smallest
   HN complaint, fix it, reply "shipped."
5. **Pin the launch tweet.** Every profile visitor for a week sees it.

## What NOT to do

- Never ask for upvotes/stars anywhere (detection = burial)
- Never post the same copy twice (rewrite the angle per room)
- Never launch Friday night or weekends for HN (dead zone)
- Never go silent for 24h mid-launch (momentum dies)
